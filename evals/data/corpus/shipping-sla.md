# Halden Freight — Shipping Service Levels

## Service tiers
Standard freight is delivered within 3 to 5 business days inside the EU. Express freight is delivered the next business day for orders booked before 14:00 CET. Same-day courier service is available in Hamburg, Rotterdam and Antwerp only.

## Tracking
Every shipment receives a tracking number when the label is created. Tracking events are updated at least every 4 hours while a shipment is in transit.

## Delays and credits
If an Express shipment arrives late, the customer receives a service credit of 50 percent of the shipping fee. Standard shipments delayed by more than 3 business days receive a 20 percent credit. Credits are not paid for delays caused by customs inspections or extreme weather.

## Weight limits
Standard parcels can weigh up to 31.5 kg. Heavier items are shipped as palletized freight with a maximum of 1,000 kg per pallet.
