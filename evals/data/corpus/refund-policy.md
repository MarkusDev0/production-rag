# Halden Freight — Refunds and Cancellations

## Cancelling a booking
Bookings can be cancelled free of charge until the shipment is picked up. After pickup, a cancellation fee of 25 EUR applies to cover handling costs.

## Refunds
Refunds for cancelled bookings are issued to the original payment method within 7 business days. Refunds for damaged goods are handled through an insurance claim instead of a direct refund.

## Insurance claims
Damage must be reported within 14 days of delivery, together with photos of the packaging and the damaged item. Basic insurance covers up to 500 EUR per shipment. Extended insurance can be purchased at booking and covers the declared value up to 25,000 EUR.

## Disputes
If a customer disagrees with a claim decision, they can request a review by the claims team lead within 30 days of the decision.
