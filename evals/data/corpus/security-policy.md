# Halden Freight — Information Security Policy

## Accounts and authentication
All employees must enable two-factor authentication on every company account. Hardware security keys are mandatory for administrators of production systems. Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are stored only in the company password manager.

## Devices
Company laptops use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the security team within 1 hour by emailing security@haldenfreight.example.

## Data classification
Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Customer shipment records are classified as Confidential. Restricted data, such as payment card details, may never be copied to personal devices or third-party AI tools.

## Access reviews
Access rights to production systems are reviewed every quarter. Accounts of people who leave the company are deactivated on their last working day.

## Incidents
Suspected security incidents must be reported immediately in the #security-incidents channel. The security team acknowledges reports within 15 minutes during business hours.
