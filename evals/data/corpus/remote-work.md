# Halden Freight — Remote Work Guidelines

## Eligibility
Office-based employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week after completing their probation period of 6 months. Warehouse and driver roles are not eligible for remote work.

## Core hours
Remote employees must be reachable between 10:00 and 15:00 CET on working days. Team meetings are scheduled inside core hours whenever possible.

## Working abroad
Working from another country is allowed for up to 20 working days per calendar year, subject to manager approval and a tax check by HR. Working from outside the EU requires approval from the legal team.

## Equipment and security
Remote employees must use a company laptop and connect through the company VPN when accessing internal systems. Public Wi-Fi may only be used with the VPN enabled.
