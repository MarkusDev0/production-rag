# Halden Freight — New Employee Onboarding

## Before day one
New hires receive their laptop by courier at least 2 days before their start date. Accounts are created by IT, and login details are sent to the personal email address on file.

## First week
Every new employee is assigned an onboarding buddy from their own team. The mandatory security awareness training must be completed within the first 5 working days. New hires meet their manager on day one to agree on goals for the probation period.

## Probation
The probation period lasts 6 months. A formal review takes place at the end of month 3 and again at the end of month 6.

## Benefits enrollment
Employees must enroll in the pension plan within 30 days of their start date. The public transport subsidy can be requested at any time through the HR portal.
