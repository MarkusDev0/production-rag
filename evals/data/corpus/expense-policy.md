# Halden Freight — Expense Policy

## Scope
This policy applies to all full-time employees and contractors who incur costs on behalf of Halden Freight.

## Submitting expenses
Expenses must be submitted through the finance portal within 30 days of the purchase date. Each claim needs an itemized receipt. Claims older than 30 days require written approval from the department head.

## Meals and travel
The daily meal allowance during business travel is 65 EUR per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable under any circumstances. Economy class is the default for flights under six hours; business class may be booked for flights longer than six hours with manager approval.

## Equipment
Employees may expense home office equipment up to 400 EUR per calendar year. Laptops and phones are provided by IT and must not be purchased personally.

## Reimbursement
Approved expenses are reimbursed with the next monthly payroll run. Corporate card holders must reconcile their statements by the 5th of each month.
